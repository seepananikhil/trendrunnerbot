How to Setup Broker & Telegram Credentials
1. Kotak Neo Trade API
  1. Log in to the Kotak Trade API Portal and copy your Consumer Key.
  2. Whitelist your server's static IP address in the Kotak portal API settings.
  3. Enter your UCC / Client ID, registered mobile (+91...), and 6-digit MPIN.
  4. Enable 2FA TOTP in Kotak Neo and paste the Base32 TOTP Secret so auto-login runs at 08:55 IST daily.
  5. Click Save & Reconnect Accounts, then verify with Test Kotak.
2. Telegram Alerts & Bot Control
  1. Open @BotFather in Telegram, run /newbot, and paste the Bot Token here.
  2. Open your newly created bot in Telegram and send /start.
  3. Click 🔍 Detect next to Chat ID to auto-fill your numeric Chat ID.
  4. Click Save & Reconnect Accounts and verify with Test Telegram.
