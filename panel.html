Broker & Telegram Connection

Credentials are stored locally on this VM. Leave any field blank to keep existing.

Where to find these credentials
  • Kotak Consumer Key: Log in to the Kotak Neo Trade API portal → My Apps → copy the Consumer Key and whitelist this VM's static IP.
  • Kotak UCC & Mobile: Your Client Code (UCC) from Kotak Neo Profile, and mobile with +91 (e.g. +919876543210).
  • MPIN & TOTP Secret: Your 6-digit trading MPIN. Enable TOTP 2FA in Kotak Neo and copy the Base32 Secret Key shown alongside the QR code.
  • Telegram Bot Token: Open @BotFather in Telegram → send /newbot → name it (e.g. My TrendRunner Bot) → copy the API token.
  • Telegram Chat ID: Send /start to your new bot, then click Detect above to fill your Chat ID automatically.
